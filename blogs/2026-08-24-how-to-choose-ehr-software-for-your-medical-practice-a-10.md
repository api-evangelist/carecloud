To choose the right EHR software for your medical practice, first map your clinical, administrative, and financial workflows. Identify your core requirements, then evaluate top EHR vendors using the same real-world scenarios. Compare usability, interoperability, security, implementation, data migration, AI capabilities, support, integrations, and total cost before making a final decision.
