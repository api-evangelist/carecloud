In 2026, ambient AI technology is changing clinical workflows by reducing administrative workload and improving providers’ satisfaction. Most healthcare providers choose medicine because they want to take care of patients, not to devote their time to documenting every patient’s notes. Yet recording and managing patient information has become a major responsibility for many clinicians.
